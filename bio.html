About me

I'm a computer science researcher who loves the real-world side of system networks. At the University of Waterloo, I'm a Visiting Researcher in Prof. Raouf Boutaba's group, where I study seamless handover and inter-PLMN mobility in 5G standalone networks. This work is supported by Canada's Department of National Defence.

I did my MSc in Computer Science under the supervision of Prof. Hanan Lutfiyya Western University. My thesis combined mobility prediction with reinforcement learning to allocate 5G resources for connected and autonomous mobility services, and turned into papers at IEEE VCC 2025 and IEEE SM 2026. Before that, I studied Applied Mathematics at the American University of Beirut as a Mastercard Foundation Scholar, which is where I fell for the mix of math and real systems that I still enjoy today.

Outside research, I teach math to school and college students, and I mentor women who are just starting out in STEM. I'm also doing a bachelor's degree in social sciences at Usul Academy, because I want to understand the human side of technology.
