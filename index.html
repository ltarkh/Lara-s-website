About

I am a researcher and developer based in Canada with a master's in Computer Science. I am currently a Visiting Researcher at the University of Waterloo in Dr. Raouf Boutaba's lab, working on research supported through Canada's Department of National Defence.

My work focuses on the intersection of algorithm design, telecommunications, and AI-driven network optimization. I am interested in practical 5G systems, mobility, slice control, and reliable service continuity.

Research Focus

My work makes next-generation mobile networks more efficient, resilient, and aware of the services they carry.

5G Mobility & Roaming

Inter-PLMN mobility, home-routed and local-breakout roaming, service continuity, and anchoring decisions under degradation.

AI-Driven Network Optimization

Predictive analytics to adapt slice resources and improve real-time service performance.

Open5GS Testbed Evaluation

Experimental workflows for validating 5G core behaviour, interruption time, dataplane anchoring, and closed-loop remediation.
