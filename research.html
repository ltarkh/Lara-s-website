Hybrid 5G Resource Allocation

Western University · 2024 – 2026 · MSc thesis

Predicting where users will move, placing virtual network functions ahead of them, and then fine-tuning slice resources in real time with reinforcement learning, for connected and autonomous mobility services.

MAGHE · IEEE VCC’25 RL-Loop · IEEE SM’26 Thesis Post

CollaboratorsZubair Md Fadlullah·Mostafa M. Fouda·Ali Chouman

Themes

01

5G mobility, handover & roaming

How sessions survive when users move across cells and operators: inter-PLMN mobility, home-routed versus local-breakout roaming, anchoring decisions, and service continuity under degradation.

inter-PLMN · 3GPP mobility · service continuity · mission-critical

02

Predictive learning for mobility management

Learning from real radio measurements to anticipate when a handover will happen and where the user will go, so the network can prepare early instead of reacting late.

sequence models · multi-task learning · measurement datasets

03

AI-driven resource allocation & slicing

Mobility-aware VNF embedding (MAGHE) and reinforcement-learning control loops (RL-Loop) that adapt slice resources in real time.

VNF embedding · network slicing · reinforcement learning

04

Experimental 5G core evaluation

Open-source testbeds (Open5GS, UERANSIM) for measuring interruption time, data-plane anchoring, and closed-loop remediation.

Open5GS · UERANSIM · testbeds
